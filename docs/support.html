Support us

Keep the journal free.

No ads, no in-app purchases, nothing to unlock.

Every feature in Lyceum is free. It works entirely on your phone and never asks you to sign up. There are no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and we'd like to keep it that way.

Building Lyceum, testing it on each new version of Android and answering bug reports all take time. If Lyceum has given you a quiet few minutes at the end of the day, a donation helps pay for that time. It's entirely optional, and every feature stays free either way.

Other ways to help: report a bug or suggest a feature, or tell a friend who might find it useful.
