How it works

  1. IRead the prompt. One short, open question a day, such as “What did you enjoy for its own sake?”
  2. IIWrite a few lines. As much or as little as you like. A mood as a word, never a score, and one thing you learned.
  3. IIIClose the book. A passage from Aristotle to end on. Tomorrow's page waits, whenever you like.

What it does

A few quiet minutes, and a way to find them again.

  • A prompt a day Twelve short questions that take turns. Edit them, reorder them, switch some off, or write your own.
  • Moods as words Heavy, Low, Even, Good or Bright, if you want one. A word for the day, never a score or a chart.
  • One thing you learned A single line, kept with the page and gathered in your weekly review.
  • Aristotle, after you save 31 passages from the Nicomachean Ethics in Rackham's translation, each checked word for word, chosen to suit the day.
  • A calendar Each month, a small dot under every day you wrote. No counter, no chain to keep.
  • Search and tags Find any page by a word or a tag, across everything you've written.
  • A weekly review What you learned, how the days felt, and the threads that ran through the week.
  • A gentle evening reminder Optional. One quiet nudge, only on days you haven't written yet, and never a word about missed days.

Private by design

What you write is for you alone.

No account, no analytics, no ads and no servers of our own. Your pages stay on your phone unless you export them or choose an encrypted backup.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, every feature, for everyone. There are no ads or in-app purchases, and the app doesn't contain ad or payment code. If you'd like to help, you can support it on Ko-fi.

What if I don't write for a while?

Nothing happens. There are no streaks to break and nothing counts the days you didn't write. Today's page is there whenever you like.

Can I change the prompts?

Yes. Edit them, put them in a different order, switch off the ones you'd rather not see, or write your own. You can bring back the originals at any time; your own prompts stay.

Does it work with Stagira?

If you use Stagira, the habit tracker, you can link a page to one of your Stagira goals. Only the goal's name and id are kept with the page, and nothing leaves your phone.

Do I need an account? What if I change phones?

No account needed. Restore your encrypted backup from your own Google Drive, or export a file and import it on the new phone. Where a day exists on both, the newer page is kept, and nothing on the phone is deleted.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. Lyceum is Android-only for now (Android 8.0 and later), and it's coming to Google Play soon.

Why “Lyceum”?

The Lyceum was the school Aristotle founded in Athens, where he taught the ideas in the Nicomachean Ethics. Lyceum keeps a little of that company at the end of each day.
