# Delete your data: Lyceum

**Lyceum – Daily Journal**, by ToolCrate, has no accounts and no servers. Everything stays on your phone,
plus an encrypted backup in your own Google Drive if you turned that on. We (the developer) never
receive any of it, so there is nothing for us to delete and no request you need to send.
You can delete all of it yourself, at any time:

## 1. Delete the data on your phone

Uninstall **Lyceum** (long-press its icon → **Uninstall**), or go to Android **Settings → Apps → Lyceum → Storage → Clear storage**. This deletes everything Lyceum keeps on your phone, straight away. To delete single pages instead, open a page and delete it.

## 2. Delete the Google Drive backup (only if you turned it on)

1. Open Lyceum → **Settings** → under *Backup to Google Drive*, tap **Delete the backup from Drive**, and confirm.

Or, without the app: in Google Drive on the web, open **Settings** (the gear) → **Manage apps**, find **Lyceum**, and choose **Delete hidden app data**.

Your backup is deleted from Google Drive straight away. Google may keep deleted data in its own
systems for a short time, as described in Google's privacy policy.

## What gets deleted

- Your journal pages: what you wrote, the prompt, an optional mood word, an optional "one thing I learned" line and tags
- An optional link from a page to a goal in the Stagira app
- Your prompts and settings, including the evening reminder time
- Your app-lock PIN (stored only as a one-way hash; never in the backup)

Nothing is kept anywhere else: Lyceum has no analytics, no crash reports and no other copies.
Exported files you saved yourself are yours to delete.
