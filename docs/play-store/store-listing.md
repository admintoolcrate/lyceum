# Store listing

Play Console → Grow → Store presence → Main store listing.

**App name** (30 characters max)

> Lyceum – Daily Journal

**Short description** (80 characters max)

> One quiet prompt a day, a few lines, and a line of Aristotle. No ads, no streaks.

**Full description**

> Lyceum is a quiet place for a few lines each day.
>
> Each day brings one short prompt, such as "What went well? What slipped? Why?" Write as much or as
> little as you like. Add a mood if you want to, as a word rather than a score, and one thing you
> learned. When you save, Lyceum shows a short passage from Aristotle's Nicomachean Ethics, chosen
> to suit what you wrote.
>
> CALM BY DESIGN
> • No streaks, no badges, no scores, no guilt
> • An optional evening reminder that stays quiet once you've written, and never mentions missed days
> • A warm paper look, in light and dark
>
> FIND YOUR WAY BACK
> • A calendar of the days you wrote
> • Search across every page, by word or tag
> • A weekly review that gathers what you learned and how the days felt
> • Prompts you can edit, reorder or replace with your own
>
> YOURS ALONE
> • Free, with no ads, no accounts and no analytics
> • Everything stays on your phone
> • App lock with a PIN, fingerprint or face
> • Export to a plain file at any time
> • An optional backup to your own Google Drive, encrypted with a passphrase only you know
>
> Works alongside Stagira: link a page to one of your Stagira goals.
>
> Quotations: Aristotle, Nicomachean Ethics, translated by H. Rackham (1926), via the Perseus Digital
> Library (CC BY-SA 4.0).
